# Customer-Behavior-Segmentation-Using-Unsupervised-Machine-Learning

## Customer Behavior Segmentation

### Project aim: 
To identify distinct groups of online shoppers based on their browsing patterns and engagement metrics using clustering algorithms, and to analyze how these groups differ in purchase conversion rates to inform targeted marketing strategies and improve the online shopping experience.

### What problem am I solving:
E-commerce businesses receive visitors with different browsing patterns. Some leave the website quickly, some spend considerable time exploring products, and others eventually make purchases. However, treating all these visitors the same makes it difficult for businesses to identify where they are losing potential customers and how to improve their marketing strategies.

My project uses unsupervised machine learning to group online shopping sessions into distinct behavioral segments. By understanding these segments and comparing their purchase rates, I aim to help businesses identify opportunities to improve customer engagement, optimize their websites, and increase conversion rates.
